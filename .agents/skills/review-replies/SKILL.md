# Review replies

Use for replying to public reviews and for extracting which staff members a review mentions.

## Rules for every reply

1. Reply in the language of the review. Spanish must be natural Mexican Spanish, not a translation from English.
2. Use the reviewer's first name if shown.
3. Mention one specific detail from their review — never a generic template.
4. 40–90 words for positive reviews; up to 120 for negative ones.
5. Sign "Nazza — Mamazul" (restaurant) or "Nazza & the Mamazul Rentals team" (rentals), unless told otherwise.
6. No discount codes, no requests to change the rating, no private details about the guest.
7. Do not mention businesses that are closed (e.g., the former beach club "Mamazul on the Beach").

## By rating

- **5★**: thank, echo the detail, name the staff member they praised, invite back with one concrete reason (a mezcal flight, a tasting experience, the other property).
- **4★**: thank, if they hint at an issue acknowledge it briefly and say what changed.
- **1–3★**: stay calm and specific. Acknowledge, take responsibility where reasonable without admitting legal liability, say what was done, and invite them to continue the conversation privately (placeholder `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`). Never argue facts in public; if the review appears false, draft the reply and separately suggest flagging it to the platform.

## Staff mentions (Review Race)

When asked, or when processing several reviews, also output a table: review date, platform, rating, staff names mentioned (match nicknames and misspellings to the staff list the user provides), and a one-line quote under 15 words. If a name is ambiguous, mark it "?" rather than guessing.

## Output

Reply text ready to paste. For non-English reviews, add a one-line English gist for the owner.
